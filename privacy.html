Template text. Have it reviewed and edit it so it matches what your site actually does before you rely on it.

Location and weather

When you search for a place or tap Use my location, the coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to look up weather. We do not store them on a server. Your last location is saved in your browser so the page can reopen where you left off.

Your hunt log

Hunt logs are stored in your browser on your device. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so export a CSV for a backup.

Ads and cookies

If ads are turned on, the page asks before loading them. Advertising partners such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads. If you decline, no ad scripts load. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Affiliate links

Some links to gear are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
